## Description

```text
Earlo shows the battery of your AirPods, Beats and Samsung Galaxy Buds on Windows 11 and Windows 10, the way your iPhone does. Open the case and a popup shows the left earbud, the right earbud and the case. Take an earbud out and your music pauses; put it back in and it plays again.

FEATURES
• Battery popup when you open the case: left, right and case, with charging state
• Live battery levels of your earbuds and headphones in the notification area (system tray)
• Automatic ear detection: pauses Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, browsers and other media when you take an earbud out, and resumes when you put it back
• See which earbud is in your ear, out of your ear or in the case
• Connect and disconnect your earbuds in one click
• A "Connected" notice in the top-right corner when your earbuds connect, with their battery levels
• Optional: connect to this PC as soon as you put the earbuds in, like switching on an iPhone
• A volume indicator when you change the volume on your earbuds
• Low battery warnings for each earbud and the case
• Starts with Windows, light and dark mode, Windows 11 design

FIX THE MUFFLED SOUND OF AIRPODS ON WINDOWS
Do your AirPods sound muffled, flat or mono on Windows, especially in Teams, Discord or Zoom? As soon as an app opens the earbuds' microphone, Windows switches them from stereo music to the Hands-Free profile, which sounds like a phone call. Earlo turns that off with one switch, so your earbuds stay in stereo. The catch: the earbuds' microphone then stops working and calls use your PC's microphone. You can switch it back at any time.

WORKS WITH
AirPods: AirPods (1st, 2nd and 3rd generation), AirPods 4, AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, AirPods Pro, AirPods Pro 2 (Lightning and USB-C), AirPods Pro 3, AirPods Max (Lightning and USB-C)
Beats: Powerbeats Pro, Powerbeats Pro 2, Powerbeats, Powerbeats3, Beats Fit Pro, Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Solo Buds, Beats Flex, BeatsX, Beats Solo3, Beats Solo 4, Beats Solo Pro, Beats Studio3, Beats Studio Pro
Samsung: Galaxy Buds, Galaxy Buds+, Galaxy Buds Live, Galaxy Buds Pro, Galaxy Buds2, Galaxy Buds2 Pro, Galaxy Buds FE, Galaxy Buds3, Galaxy Buds3 Pro, Galaxy Buds3 FE, Galaxy Buds Core
Galaxy Buds show their exact battery levels once they are connected to your PC.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Your PC needs Bluetooth (Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth 4.0 or newer).
• The app is in English and Dutch.
• The Store version cannot switch noise control (noise cancellation, transparency): that needs a driver, and Store apps may not install drivers.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Earlo sends your data nowhere.

Earlo was called PodPilot before. It is open source: https://github.com/kylianxdev/Earlo
Buying it in the Store supports its development.

AirPods and Beats are trademarks of Apple Inc. Galaxy Buds is a trademark of Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. Earlo is not affiliated with Apple or Samsung.
```
